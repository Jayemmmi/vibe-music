What is Pop Music?

Pop music is a popular genre known for catchy melodies, memorable choruses, and songs that are easy to listen to.

History

Pop music developed from earlier popular music and became especially influential during the 20th century. It continued to evolve with new technology and changing musical trends.

Characteristics

  • Catchy melodies
  • Memorable choruses
  • Verse-and-chorus structures
  • Modern music production
  • Strong focus on vocals
