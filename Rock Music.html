🎸Rock Music🎸

What is Rock?

Rock music is a genre known for strong rhythms, electric guitars, powerful vocals, and energetic performances.

History

Rock music developed during the 20th century from earlier musical styles and became one of the most influential genres in popular music.

Characteristics

  • Strong guitar riffs
  • Heavy drum beats
  • Bass guitar
  • Powerful vocals
  • Energetic performances

  • Queen
  • AC/DC
