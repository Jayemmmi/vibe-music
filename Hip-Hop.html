What is Hip-Hop?

Hip-hop is a cultural movement and musical genre known for rapping, rhythmic beats, DJing, and creative expression.

History

Hip-hop originated in the Bronx, New York, during the 1970s and later spread throughout the United States and around the world.

Elements of Hip-Hop

  • MCing or rapping
  • DJing
  • Graffiti art
  • Breakdancing

Famous Artists

  • Tupac Shakur
  • The Notorious B.I.G.
  • Eminem
  • Jay-Z
  • Kendrick Lamar
